## Projects (all listed in profile)

1. **Wezesha Impact | 2024–2025 | Ongoing**. ILO-funded BRMM (Better Regional Migration Management)
   Project. SIYB training to Ugandan returnees. Contract UGX 16,000,000.
2. **Uganda Wildlife Authority (UWA) | 2024–2025 | Ongoing**. Climate change risks, Disaster Risk
   Reduction and Climate Mitigation Plan, climate resilience in protected areas. Funded directly by
   UWA. Contract UGX 49,250,000.
3. **Finn Church Aid (DRDIP Projects) | 2019–2024 | Ongoing and Completed**. Development Response to
   Displacement Impacts Project: market risks training (UGX 66,660,000, 2024); feasibility studies and
   irrigation schemes Koboko (UGX 23,000,000, 2023); community planning guides land tenure
   (2020–2021); irrigation equipment supply (UGX 195,000,000, 2019).
4. **Rural Women and Youth Empowerment Centre (RWAYEC) | 2014–2020 | Completed**. Tororo District:
   climate risk training 2020 (UGX 87,000,000, EU-funded); agribusiness training cassava/potato 2014
   (UGX 120,000,000, Government of Uganda, 300+ farmer groups).
5. **Child Care and Youth Empowerment Foundation | 2018 | Completed**. Strategic plan. Funded by
   Private Sector Foundation Uganda (UGX 19,337,400).
6. **Private Sector Foundation Uganda | 2019 | Completed**. Procurement and supply of irrigation
   equipment.
7. **UGEFA (conclusion section)**. EU funded, implemented by Adelphi Research GmbH with SEED and
   Finding XYZ. Supported 50+ SMEs (clean energy, green manufacturing, sustainable tourism, waste
   management, sustainable transport); 20 of 100+ applicants selected for financing support.

## Method

3 phases (How we work page): Diagnose and Co-Design, Deliver and Empower, Monitor, Learn and Adapt.
7 steps (How it works page): Participatory Needs Assessment; Co-Development of Tailored Action Plans;
Collaborative Implementation; Continuous Monitoring and Adaptive Learning; Transparent Communication
& Reporting; Measurable Outcomes and Sustainability; Flexible Field-Based Delivery.

consolidates everything into the 3 phases and rewrites steps 5 and 6 distinctly.
