# PRODUCT.md: brand context

**Africa Management Consult Limited (AMC)** is a Ugandan consulting firm (incorporated 2011,
Reg. No. 138382) delivering enterprise development, training, research and project management for
donors, NGOs, government and corporates across Uganda and East Africa.

Register: professional, grounded, evidence-led. The visitor is deciding whether to hire AMC
(mode: Persuade). Trust first: real field photography, named funders, verified numbers.

Anti-references (never do these):

- Generic AI-slop styling: purple/blue gradient blobs, gradient washes, glassmorphism used
  as decoration rather than structure, pill tags over hero photos.
- Stock skyscrapers and glass towers (the old profile cover; explicitly retired).
- Invented testimonials, inflated or duplicated statistics, placeholder names.

Signature language (updated for the Deloitte-informed direction):

- Arch-shaped photo masks and the scallop border from the brand identity.
- Solid navy `#334269` + bright blue `#4196cf`, cool off-white paper. Solid colour fields only;
  depth comes from layered surfaces (frosted glass panels, soft shadows), never from gradients.
- Controlled morphic surfaces (`.glass`, `.glass-light`) over photos and ink fields.
- Smooth motion: condensed sticky header, hero entrance stagger, scroll reveals, card lifts,
  animated accordion. All disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Photo-led editorial calm: large type, generous whitespace, quiet motion.

Primary conversion: **Request a proposal**. Secondary: capability statement download (Phase 3),
click-to-call and WhatsApp.
